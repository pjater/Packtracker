How it works

Up and running in 30 seconds.

No setup, no account, no nonsense.

01
Open PackTracker

Go to packtracker.onrender.com in any modern browser. That's it — no install needed.

02
Add your packs

Create a profile for your modpack and start adding mods, shaders, and resource packs.

03
Stay organized

Your data is saved locally. Come back anytime — everything is exactly where you left it.
